# Rebuilding a customer map from existing research

Use this procedure when the user requests a new segmentation pass over saved material.
It produces a map of the reviewed corpus, not an exhaustive market map. Preserve the
user's scope and any already authorized changes to the catalog.

Read [formation rules](formation-rules.md) and use the mandatory
[generator–critic debate](generator-critic.md) for the map and its boundaries.
Case extraction workers are additional roles; the generator cannot review its own map.

## Frame and responsibilities

The lead defines the product boundary, what makes a customer practice recognizable,
and what decision a segment boundary should change. Begin with who uses or could use
the product, their practice, responsibility and circumstances. Explain differences
through jobs, success criteria, purchase or implementation. Do not start with the old
card count, a target number of segments, function names or parameter permutations.

Pin graph revision, research revision and factRevision. Inventory materials by type,
source and case ID through the shared research reader. Check freshness, field origins
and completed case reviews. Use saved individual facts; reopen originals for a named
gap, ambiguous attribution, contradiction or audit. Unknown is a valid review outcome.

If parallel agents are authorized, partition work into disjoint case lists and give
each worker its own output folder. Keep derivative reports and team statements in a
separate reconciliation task. Pick models from the available catalog and the user's
instructions; do not hard-code a model into the methodology. Workers propose findings;
the lead owns boundary decisions and transactional writes. Rebalance unfinished case
lists explicitly, preserving checkpoints and avoiding duplicate review counts.

## Worker outputs

For every assigned case, record its stable case/person/episode IDs, review outcome,
recognizable practice, product scope, candidate memberships, exact record/source-unit
references, time, attribution and unresolved questions. Distinguish explicit membership
from a tentative inference. Number of clients or online teaching alone does not prove
self-employment, individual lessons, an organization or purchasing authority.

For each candidate, describe who, inclusion/exclusion signs, context, primary and
additional jobs, criteria, roles and customer journey. Explain why a distinct offer,
product choice or implementation might follow. Preserve contrary cases. Do not fill
unknown payment, discovery, priority or renewal fields from a plausible story.

Maintain a reconciliation ledger for every prior candidate: retain, rename, merge,
split, defer or retire, with a reason and source. A prior AI report remains a hypothesis;
a retelling of an interview is not an additional independent case. General expert
approval of an old report does not endorse every claim in it.

## Lead synthesis and review

1. Compare candidates across streams before fixing names and nesting. Familiar groups
   organize navigation; each segment describes a coherent customer practice. Add a
   subsegment only when its narrower circumstances materially change value, criteria,
   purchase or implementation. Allow overlapping practices without forcing each case
   into exactly one bucket.
2. Separate observed practices, team descriptions and potential audiences. Audit the
   positive membership decisions, ambiguous cases, exclusions and product boundaries.
   A mentioned product name does not establish the respondent's organizational role.
   A membership must cite the passage that establishes its boundary, not merely a
   valid record from the same case. Apply the same boundary to interviews, stories
   and surveys. Do not transfer evidence between suspected duplicate anonymous forms
   until identity is established. Plans to launch a course are not launched programs;
   a tariff name does not establish a different product's adoption or payment.
3. Check the complete assigned inventory. Report reviewed, insufficient and missing
   cases separately. Material counts are not unique people, market shares or demand.
   Frequency does not establish criterion priority. Preserve unknown ranking.
4. Give the merged proposal and decision ledger to the independent critic, using the
   bounded debate and explicit responses/resolutions. Check objections against referenced records;
   a schema check or a worker's completion message alone is not a semantic review.
   If the user prohibited delegation or a critic is unavailable, retain an unreviewed
   draft and the missing check; never silently claim the independent gate passed.

## Persistence and completion

Save the readable map, coverage ledger, decisions and source bindings first. Each
customer-profile statement retains its own basis and attributable sources. Derived
interpretations remain hypotheses even when the underlying observation is sourced.
Use typed SynthesisRevision and MembershipAssertion records with reviewed field
provenance for the new boundaries; do not inherit membership or support from a group,
parent segment or an old job-based cut. Keep original records and correction history.

Write graph, research synthesis, canonical move and completion through graph-write,
with both current revision guards. Preserve IDs for unchanged identities; archive
replaced profiles with a sourced reason. Inspect the dry run, then read back the saved
map and relevant research summary. When the console is part of the task, verify its
actual profiles, counts, evidence statuses and source links. Finish by stating what
the map supports deciding and the most consequential unresolved question.
